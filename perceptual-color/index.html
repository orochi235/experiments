Triangle vertices are B(Lmin, cbot), P(Lpeak, cpeak), T(Lmax, ctop). Interior is a linear-RGB barycentric blend of the three corner colors at the Reference hue, which also drives the L and C slider tracks above. Dashed tracks show each thumb's bounded slide range. Small squares on the curve mark each palette index from the Indices control below at its (L, C) sample — their spacing along the envelope follows the L curve gamma.

Categorical / chart colors

A separate set of standalone hues — for charts, multi-series visualizations, or anywhere you need a small set of distinct colors that aren't part of a graded family. Each marker is one color. Drag horizontally to change its hue. Hold Shift while dragging to translate all hues together. Click empty space on the band to add a marker. Drag a swatch onto another to reorder. The mid-L / peak-C from the shared params is used to render the swatches.
